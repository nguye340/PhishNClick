📖 Learn Before You Play

🎮 About the Game

This interactive game helps you practice identifying phishing emails and safe online behaviors. You’ll review emails and decide whether to Catch (keep) or Drop (avoid) them.

🕹️ How to Play

  • 📧 Click on floating email icons to read the message.
  • ✔️ If the email is safe and legitimate, click Catch.
  • 🚨 If it’s a phishing email, click Drop to protect yourself.
  • 🔍 Be careful: Clicking malicious links inside an email will take you to a phishing warning & education page.

📈 How to Score High

  • +10 points for catching genuine emails.
  • +10 points for dropping phishing emails.
  • -5 points for catching a phishing email or dropping a genuine one.
  • +5 bonus points for catching tricky phishing tactics like fake links or urgency messages.

🚨 How to Spot a Phishing Email

  • 👀 Check the Sender: Look for misspellings, strange domains, or fake company names.
  • 🔗 Hover Over Links: Before clicking, hover your mouse over the link to preview where it actually goes. Phishing emails often use URLs that look similar to real sites but have extra words or misspellings.
  • ⚠️ Look for Urgent Language: Phrases like “Act now!” or “Your account will be suspended!” are red flags.
  • 📎 Avoid Attachments: Be cautious of attachments you weren’t expecting.
  • 📝 Check for Bad Grammar: Many phishing emails have poor spelling and grammar mistakes.

💡 Understand the Email Content

When reading an email:

  • Does it seem too good to be true?
  • Is it asking for personal information?
  • Is it asking you to urgently verify or log in?
  • Is the link domain legit? (e.g., paypal.com vs paypal-support-help.com)
  • If unsure — better to Drop it!

🛡️ Avoid Link Traps

  • Always hover your mouse over any link before clicking.
  • Never click on a link that leads to a domain you don't recognize.
  • Check for misspellings or extra characters (e.g., secure-paypall.com)
  • When in doubt, drop the email or report it.
